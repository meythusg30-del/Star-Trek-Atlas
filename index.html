Star Trek Online

A free-to-play MMORPG that picks up decades after the last TNG film. You create your own captain, command a starship and crew of bridge officers, and fight in both space and on the ground.

The story begins in 2409. The Romulan homeworld is gone, the Klingon Empire and the Federation are at war again, and the Borg are back. Over sixteen years, the game has grown into a sprawling continuation of Trek, with many actors from the shows returning to voice their characters.

Space combat is about positioning your ship: keep your strongest shield facing the enemy, line up your weapon arcs, and fire bridge officer abilities at the right moment. Ground missions play like a squad shooter with your away team.

Try the power panel

In Star Trek Online, your ship splits its reactor between four subsystems. Every point you put in one is a point another doesn’t get. Drag the sliders or try a preset.

    What’s next

    Where things stand as of October 2026.

    A new film

    Paramount confirmed a new theatrical Star Trek film is in development, directed by Jonathan Goldstein and John Francis Daley. It will have an all-new cast of characters, will not continue the Chris Pine films, and is set further in the future than any Star Trek story so far.

    No release date has been announced, and the script was still being written as of summer 2026.

    The game

    Season 37, “Rediscovered,” arrived on PC on September 22 and reaches PlayStation and Xbox in November 2026. More remakes of the game’s early missions are planned.

    On television, Strange New Worlds and Starfleet Academy are both set to wrap up in 2027.
